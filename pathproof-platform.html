A report is only trusted when others agree

PATHPROOF separates reliable updates from guesses, so nobody is sent to a broken lift.

  1. ReportAnyone flags a blocked ramp or lift, with an optional photo.
  2. CorroborateNearby people confirm or dispute. Three confirmations mean verified.
  3. RerouteBlocked facilities are skipped and the next usable route is shown.

Questions people ask

How do you know a report is true?

One report stays unconfirmed. When three people agree it becomes verified, and old reports expire until someone confirms them again.

Can the explanations be wrong?

Yes. They are guidance, and each one tells you what to verify with the official source or a professional.

Does it work offline?

Not in this prototype. Saving plans and explanations for offline use is on the roadmap.

Is it free?

Individuals use it free. We plan partnerships with hospitals, stations and local bodies who want to keep their facilities information accurate.

    Check a message before you tap it.

    Paste an SMS or WhatsApp message. PATHPROOF looks for common scam signs and tells you what to do. Nothing is sent anywhere, the check runs in your browser.

    Scams to watch for

    Bank blocked or KYC updateBanks do not block accounts by SMS or ask for KYC through links.
    Electricity or gas disconnectionVerify on the official app or helpline printed on your bill.
    Digital arrest and customs callsNo agency arrests people over a phone or video call. Hang up.
    UPI request or QR to receive moneyYou never need a PIN to receive money. Decline the request.
    Easy online jobs and tasksReal jobs do not ask you to pay first or to like and subscribe for pay.

    This is a rule-based checker for common signs. It cannot prove a message is safe. If unsure, contact the organisation using the number on its official website or your card. Report fraud at 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
